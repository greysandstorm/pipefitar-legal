# PipeFitAR Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026
**Developer:** Unitus LLC ("we", "us")
**Contact:** [support@unitus.dev](mailto:support@unitus.dev)

PipeFitAR is a pipe-layout tool for iPhone. It works entirely on your device. **We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.**

## What the app uses, and where it stays

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | Live augmented reality so you can place pipe points on walls, floors and equipment | Processed on your iPhone by Apple's ARKit in real time. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere. |
| **Room scan (AR world map)** | Only when you save a job, so it can reopen in the same physical spot | Saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. Never uploaded. |
| **Your runs, saved jobs, tape-test calibration and settings** | So your work and calibration are still there next time | Saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. Never uploaded. |

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections of its own.

## Sharing

If you tap **Share** on a cut list or iso sketch, the iOS share sheet sends it only where you choose (Messages, Mail, AirDrop and so on). From then on, that app's privacy policy applies.

## TestFlight beta

If you install PipeFitAR through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports, usage data and feedback you submit, and share them with us under [Apple's TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/sren/terms.html). We use that only to fix and improve the app.

## Deleting your data

Delete saved jobs inside the app (Saved Jobs → swipe to delete), or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Nothing remains with us, because we never had it.

## Children

PipeFitAR is a trade tool, not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

We don't process personal data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, export or sell. If you have questions, email [support@unitus.dev](mailto:support@unitus.dev) and we'll answer within 30 days.

## Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll update this policy and change the effective date before that version ships.
